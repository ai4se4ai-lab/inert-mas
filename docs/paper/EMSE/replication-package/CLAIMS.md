# Claims and evidence

Every quantitative statement of the manuscript, where it comes from and how to check it. Numbers
in **Sections 7 and 8 and in the abstract, introduction, discussion and conclusion are not typed by
hand**: they are written into `main.tex` between `%<<name>>` and `%<</name>>` markers by
[analysis/fill_paper.py](../../../../analysis/fill_paper.py), from tables produced by
[analysis/make_assets.py](../../../../analysis/make_assets.py) and prose produced by
[analysis/paper_text.py](../../../../analysis/paper_text.py) from `paper/numbers.json`. The
numbers of Section 3 are written the same way by the taxonomy pipeline.

Verification commands (all from the repository root, none modifies the repository):

| Key | Command | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[T]** | `.venv/bin/pytest` | ~1 min |
| **[C]** | `python docs/paper/EMSE/replication-package/scripts/check_claims.py` (independent stdlib recount) | ~5 s |
| **[R]** | `bash docs/paper/EMSE/replication-package/scripts/verify_results.sh` (regenerate and compare) | ~2 min |
| **[X]** | `bash docs/paper/EMSE/replication-package/scripts/verify_taxonomy.sh` (replay the taxonomy) | ~10 s |
